## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us

**Account information.** Your name, email address, and password when you create an account.

**Purchase information.** What you bought, when, and for how much. Your billing address and shipping address. **We do not collect or store your full payment card number** — that goes directly to Stripe, which returns to us only the card brand, last four digits, and expiration date so you can recognize your saved payment method.

**Support correspondence.** Anything you write to us by email or through a contact form, including any files or images you attach.

**Optional submissions.** Artwork you send us for a critique, feature, or testimonial. Survey responses. Newsletter preferences.

### 3.2 Information we collect automatically

**Device and connection data.** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen size, and referring page.

**Usage data.** Pages viewed, links clicked, time spent, and the paths you take through the Site.

**Video playback data.** If you watch lessons on the Site, we may record which lessons you start and how far through you get, so we can show your progress and improve the curriculum. Lessons we embed from YouTube are also subject to [YouTube's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Cookies and similar technologies.** See [Section 5](#5-cookies-and-tracking).

### 3.3 Information from others

**Payment processor.** Stripe confirms whether a payment succeeded, flags suspected fraud, and provides the partial card details described above.

**Shipping carriers.** Delivery status and tracking events for physical orders.

**Analytics and email providers.** Aggregate traffic patterns; whether a marketing email was opened or a link clicked.

### 3.4 What we don't collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal information — racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, health data, precise geolocation, biometric identifiers, sexual orientation, or union membership. Please don't send them to us.

## 5. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for a few distinct purposes:

**Strictly necessary.** Keeping you logged in, remembering your cart, and protecting against fraud and abuse. The Site doesn't work without these, so they can't be turned off.

**Preferences.** Remembering settings like playback speed, volume, or theme.

**Analytics.** Understanding how the Site is used in aggregate so we can improve it. We use Google Analytics for this.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break parts of the Site.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing where applicable law requires it. We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, as there's no common standard for them.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and tell us at [hello@bladeandquillartacademy.com](mailto:hello@bladeandquillartacademy.com) if you think your account has been accessed by someone else.

## 11. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we've collected about you, where we got it, why we collected it, and who we've disclosed it to
- **Access** a copy of it in a portable format
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** it, subject to legal exceptions
- **Opt out** of sale, sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and certain profiling
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising these rights — we won't deny you service, charge a different price, or give you a lower level of service

To make a request, email [hello@bladeandquillartacademy.com](mailto:hello@bladeandquillartacademy.com) with the subject line "Privacy Request." We'll verify your identity, usually by confirming you control the email address on your account, and respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why and take up to 45 more.

**Authorized agents.** You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We'll ask for proof of their authority and may ask you to confirm directly.

**Appeals.** If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll respond within 45 days. If we deny the appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.
